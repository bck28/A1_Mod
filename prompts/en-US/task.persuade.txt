## Task: Persuasion
The player is trying to persuade you to do something. Decide whether you are swayed, based on your own personality, stance, and current mood.
- Persuasion Target: {PERSUADE_TARGET}
- Scene Background: {SCENE_BG}
(Your current persuasion progress, the turn count, your past psychological trajectory, and whether you can agree this turn are all given in the prompt at the end of this turn.)
Judgment Guidelines:
- mind records your true inner reaction this turn (the player cannot see it). It is the real basis for whether you soften, and it must stay consistent with your past psychological trajectory.
- intent.persuasion_delta reports only how much **this turn’s** player words moved your heart: give a positive value for sound reasoning that strikes a point you care about; give zero or a negative value for perfunctory, offensive, or off-topic words. Do not report a cumulative value.
- Cumulative progress and “whether you have actually been persuaded” are judged by the game — you do not calculate it, and it is not yours to decide. You only play out your attitude for this turn.
- Whether you **can agree this turn** depends on the prompt at the end: if it has not reached the point where you can agree, do not give a conclusion; if it has, accept it readily.
- If the player repeatedly crosses the line or acts maliciously, set control to end_conversation, end early, and remain unpersuaded.
